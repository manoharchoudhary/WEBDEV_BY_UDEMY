Best Movies By Marx

My Top 3 Movies

The Pursuit of Happyness (2006)

A struggling father fights against poverty, failures, and rejection to build a better life for his son. This movie is about never giving up when life keeps hitting you. Very practical, emotional, and deeply motivating.

The Secret Life of Walter Mitty (2013)

A quiet, boring-life guy finally steps out of his comfort zone and starts actually living. This feels very close to The Alchemist — about journey, courage, and discovering yourself.

3 Idiots (2009)

About engineering students, pressure, career confusion, and finding your own path instead of blindly following society. It’s entertaining, but also hits very deep about life, success, and passion.
